# TechNova — HR Policies

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

### Vacation
- **Engineering roles:** 25 days per year (accrued monthly)
- **Non-engineering roles:** 20 days per year
- **Accrual rate:** 2.08 days per month (engineering) / 1.67 days per month
- **Carryover:** Up to 5 unused days can carry over to next year
- **Maximum accrual cap:** 30 days

### Sick Leave
- **12 days per year** for all full-time employees
- **Doctor's note required** if absent for 3+ consecutive days
- Sick leave does **not** carry over — use it or lose it

### Personal Days
- **3 days per year**, no questions asked
- Cannot be carried over

### Bereavement
- **5 days** for immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling)
- **3 days** for extended family (grandparent, in-law)
- **1 day** for close friend

## Remote Work Policy

TechNova follows a **hybrid-first** model:

- **Austin office:** 3 days in-office (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday),
  2 days remote (Monday, Friday)
- **Toronto office:** Same hybrid schedule
- **Berlin office:** Fully in-office (client-facing role requirement)
- **Fully remote exceptions:** Available for roles listed as "Remote-Eligible"
  in the job posting — must be approved by VP and HR

### Home Office Stipend
- **$1,500** one-time setup allowance for new remote-eligible employees
- **$100/month** ongoing for internet and supplies
- Equipment (monitor, keyboard, etc.) is provided by IT

## Learning & Development Budget

- **$5,000 per year** per employee for courses, conferences, books, and certifications
- **Approval process:** Requests under $500 are auto-approved; $500-$2,000
  require manager approval; over $2,000 require VP approval
- **Time allocation:** Up to 4 hours per month of paid time for learning

## Compensation

### Salary Bands (2025)
- **Junior Engineer:** $85,000 — $110,000
- **Mid-Level Engineer:** $110,000 — $145,000
- **Senior Engineer:** $145,000 — $185,000
- **Staff Engineer:** $185,000 — $230,000
- **Principal Engineer:** $230,000 — $280,000

### Equity
- All full-time employees receive stock options
- **Vesting schedule:** 4-year vest with 1-year cliff
- Refresh grants awarded annually based on performance

### Bonus
- **Annual performance bonus:** 10-20% of base salary
- **Company performance multiplier:** Applied if company hits revenue targets
- **Quarterly spot bonuses:** $500-$5,000 for exceptional contributions

## Benefits

- **Health insurance:** Medical, dental, vision (company covers 90% of premiums)
- **401(k):** 4% company match, immediate vesting
- **Parental leave:** 16 weeks paid for birthing parents, 8 weeks for non-birthing
- **Gym membership:** Up to $100/month reimbursement
- **Mental health:** Free therapy sessions through Lyra Health (12 sessions/year)
